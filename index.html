About us

EUCF mission and purpose

Europe today is going through profound ideological and geopolitical transformations. Thirty-five years after the collapse of the USSR, society faces new challenges that require a strong spiritual and moral foundation.

The Ukrainian evangelical community — both in Ukraine and among the multi-million diaspora in EU countries — has a unique potential to demonstrate an evangelical approach to the spiritual renewal, unity and consolidation of all of Europe.

The event is held under the auspices of the Pray for Europe Foundation in partnership with the National Prayer Breakfast Movement, leading evangelical unions of Ukraine and European Christian alliances.
